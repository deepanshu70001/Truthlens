# Social impact methodology

The engine performs exact-token matches for five possible content categories: communal/religious themes, public health, political stability, financial safety, and child safety. A match is a cue for review, not a finding of harm, falsity, intent, or severity. It does not produce numerical risk or severity scores, audience estimates, or observed-outcome claims.

Where a matched topic overlaps an extracted Phase 3 claim, the result links its claim ID and available Phase 3 verification status/evidence IDs. Verification status is not converted into a social harm severity. Image authenticity does not establish caption truth. Urgency wording is separately exposed as detected tokens, never as proof of misinformation. OCR omissions and language coverage limit all matches. Observed impact remains `UNAVAILABLE` absent outcome data.

The category taxonomy is politically neutral and does not target individuals or sensitive groups. Category matches must be interpreted in context, including negation, quotation, satire, and benign discussion.
