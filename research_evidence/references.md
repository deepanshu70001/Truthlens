## 1. Deepfake & Synthetic Image Detection

1. **Bird, J. J., & Lotfi, A. (2024).** *CIFAKE: Image Classification and Explainable Identification of AI-Generated Synthetic Images.* IEEE Access, 12, 15600–15612.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Primary dataset source for baseline experiments (`data/external/cifake/`). DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3357564.
2. **Zhai, X., Mustafa, B., Kolesnikov, A., & Beyer, L. (2023).** *Sigmoid Loss for Language Image Pre-Training (SigLIP).* Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 11975–11986.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Foundation architecture for primary detector (`prithivMLmods/Deepfake-Real-Class-Siglip2`). arXiv:2303.15343.
3. **Wang, S. Y., Wang, O., Zhang, R., Owens, A., & Efros, A. A. (2020).** *CNN-Generated Images Are Surprisingly Easy to Spot... for Now.* Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 8695–8704.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Theoretical justification for CNN-from-scratch baseline detecting high-frequency generative artifacts.
4. **Frank, J., Eisenhofer, T., Schönherr, L., Fischer, A., Kolossa, D., & Holz, T. (2020).** *Leveraging Frequency Analysis for Deep Fake Image Recognition.* International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), PMLR, 3247–3258.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Grounding for frequency-domain analysis and JPEG grid boundary checks.
5. **Corvi, R., Cozzolino, D., Zingarini, G., Poggi, G., Nagano, K., & Verdoliva, L. (2023).** *On the Detection of Synthetic Images Generated by Diffusion Models.* IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 1–5.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Establishes cross-generator domain shift and concept drift risks.

## 2. Explainable AI & Visual Forensics

6. **Selvaraju, R. R., Cogswell, M., Das, A., Vedaldi, A., Parikh, D., & Batra, D. (2017).** *Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-Based Localization.* Proceedings of the IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 618–626.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Core algorithm implemented in `backend/app/services/xai/gradcam.py`.
7. **Krawetz, N. (2007).** *A Picture's Worth... Digital Image Analysis and Forensics.* Hacker Factor Solutions, 6(2), 2.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Foundational specification for Error Level Analysis (ELA) resave differential methodology.
8. **Farid, H. (2009).** *Image Forgery Detection: A Survey.* IEEE Signal Processing Magazine, 26(2), 16–25.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Standard reference for noise variance analysis and digital splicing detection.
9. **Zauner, C. (2010).** *Implementation and Benchmarking of Perceptual Image Hash Functions.* Master's Thesis, Upper Austria University of Applied Sciences.
   - `[VERIFIED]` Algorithm specification for pHash, dHash, and aHash implementations in `backend/app/services/hashing/service.py`.

## 3. Multimodal Misinformation & Regional Context (India)

10. **Garimella, K., & Tyson, G. (2019).** *WhatsApp, Misinformation, and the 2019 Indian General Election.* Proceedings of the International AAAI Conference on Web and Social Media (ICWSM).
    - `[VERIFIED]` Contextual foundation for understanding encrypted image-forwarding dynamics in India.
11. **Gupta, P., Anand, A., & Kumaraguru, P. (2022).** *FactDrill: A Multimodal Fact-Checked Dataset for Indian Political Discourse.* Precog Research Group, IIIT-Delhi.
    - `[CONTEXTUAL]` Audited in `docs/VIRALITY_DATA.md` for regional claim verification.
12. **Shu, K., Mahudeswaran, D., Wang, S., Lee, D., & Liu, H. (2020).** *FakeNewsNet: A Data Repository with News Content, Social Context, and Dynamic Information for Studying Fake News on Social Media.* Big Data, 8(3), 171–188.
    - `[VERIFIED]` Audited for virality and social network propagation limitations.
13. **Nielsen, I. G., & McConville, R. (2022).** *MuMiN: A Large-Scale Multilingual Multimodal Fact-Checked Misinformation Social Network Dataset.* SIGIR '22, 3141–3153.
    - `[VERIFIED]` Reference for multilingual knowledge graph claim-tweet structures.
